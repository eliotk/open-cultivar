  Harvest Moon is a hybrid (F1) specialty tomato that produces firm, golden fruits weighing 7–10 oz with a beautiful red blush interior. It offers the flavor and appearance of heirloom varieties but with fewer defects, better uniformity, and a higher yield. The indeterminate plants are compact with a balanced habit, making them suitable for basket-weave trellising. It matures in approximately 75 days and has high resistance to Late Blight.
