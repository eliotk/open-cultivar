---
title: Chateaurenard
species_link: Cabbage
description: >-
  Chateaurenard is a beloved French heirloom variety, dating back to the mid-19th century in southern France. It produces abundant, tender crops of unique green-white, cone-shaped heads. This variety is remarkably versatile, thriving in both the cool early spring and the warmth of summer. As one of the rare open-pollinated cabbages preserved over generations, it offers a distinctively sweet flavor profile perfect for fresh salads and slaws, yet robust enough for stir-fries or a quick summer sauerkraut.
