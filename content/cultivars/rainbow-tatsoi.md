  ### General Information
  * **Maturity:** 21 days (baby leaf); 45 days (full size)
  * **Type:** Open-Pollinated (OP)
  * **Characteristics:** Cold and bolt tolerant.

  ### Description
  This variety forms full, slightly varied heads of savoyed leaves. It is characterized by **pink-purple stems** and **ombré leaf coloring**, where the purple pigmentation of the outer leaves fades to green at the center. Exposure to colder temperatures intensifies the leaf color, resulting in an **intensely dark purple** hue. The leaves are tender and suitable for use in stir-fries, stews, or hearty salads.

  ### Origin and Breeding
  Rainbow Tatsoi was bred by **Dan Brisebois of Tourne-Sol Cooperative Farm**. The variety originated from an **accidental cross** between a Yukina Savoy and a Pink Petiole Mix. Following this initial cross, Brisebois conducted selection over several generations to stabilize the round leaf shape, purple color, and pink/purple stems.

  *For the complete story of the breeding process, refer to the breeder's account: [How I Bred Rainbow Tatsoi](https://danbrisebois.com/2019/02/07/how-i-bred-rainbow-tatsoi/)*
