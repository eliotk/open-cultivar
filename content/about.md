# About Open Cultivar

Open Cultivar is a community-driven project dedicated to documenting and sharing information about various plant cultivars. Our mission is to provide an accessible and comprehensive resource for gardeners, farmers, and plant enthusiasts.

We believe in open knowledge and collaboration. Everyone is welcome to contribute their observations, photos, experiences with different cultivars, and links to related info and resources.
