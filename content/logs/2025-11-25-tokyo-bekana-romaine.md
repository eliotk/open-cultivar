Tanner mentions that Tokyo Bekana is a crop that he calls ["Tokyo Bekana Romaine"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJxdybvP6Hc&t=1266s) to market it effectively. He explains that it's a combination of bok choy and cabbage, and despite the unusual name, it sells ["like crazy"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJxdybvP6Hc&t=1270s). He also notes that chefs are even forming menus around it, which he finds ["pretty cool"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJxdybvP6Hc&t=1501s). He shows an example of the Tokyo Bekana as a direct-sown seedling that will grow into a head and be sold as ["Romaine"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJxdybvP6Hc&t=1482s).
